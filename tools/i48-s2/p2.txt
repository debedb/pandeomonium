Panel: Same lamplit evening. Close on the table between them: VARYA's hand has just withdrawn after placing the large ring of household keys on the table; her face composed and hard. LEVENE, at the side, mouth half open as if about to say congratulations, sees the keys and her face and stops. LOPAKHIN's smile lingers a moment too long; RANEVSKAYA, seated, has lowered her eyes.
