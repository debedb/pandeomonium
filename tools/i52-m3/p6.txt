Panel: Wide night street in falling snow, seen from across the street. On the left, the PLAYER still holds the lit stage door open. In the middle of the street the two couples part: VERA and NORM walk off to the left, MARIS and NILES walk off to the right. On the near pavement, far apart from each other, two ragged beggars sit against the wall, each alone with a small wooden collection box at his feet: ROS at the left end, GUIL at the right end. Norm tosses a coin toward Rosencrantz; Maris, with a gloved flick, tosses a coin toward Guildenstern; both coins glint in mid-air. Between the beggars, WILSON's portable section of cedar fence stands on the pavement, his eyes and bucket hat peeking over it. On top of the gas lamp perches OWL, holding in its beak a sealed envelope stamped with a muted post horn. Leave space above the fence for speech. Only these seven people and the owl.
