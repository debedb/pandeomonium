Panel: One wide continuous image. The left third is the emptied estate room: the locked front door, FIRS small on the couch, the paper cup on the side table. To the right the estate's wall simply gives way, without any frame or portal, into a vast infinite library of towering bookcases, warm lamplight, rolling ladders (exactly like the library reference). In the library, PRESENTER steps in, takes in the old servant, and starts arranging himself into a presenter's stance: sympathetic expression, one hand raised, about to explain. Near a bookcase CAINE stands quietly; HOPPER stands beside him holding her mug. Deeper in the library, small: READERS. No other people.
